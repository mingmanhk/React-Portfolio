What this site collects

This site uses GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-focused analytics service, to count page views. It records the page visited, referring site, browser and operating system type, screen size, and approximate country.

It does not use cookies, does not collect names, email addresses, or IP addresses for tracking, and does not follow you across other websites.

What this site does not do

There are no contact forms, accounts, advertising trackers, or data sales. If you email me, I use your message only to reply.

Hosting
